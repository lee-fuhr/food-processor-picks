Best food processors for 2026

Quick answer

The Cuisinart DFP-14BCCY remains the best food processor for most households, offering a smart balance of capacity, power, and reliability at a reasonable price.

If you need more power and a larger capacity, the Breville Sous Chef is the premium choice; for tight budgets, the Hamilton Beach 70750 gets the job done; and for tiny kitchens, the KitchenAid mini processor is a space-saving gem.

A food processor can chop, slice, shred, purée, and knead in a fraction of the time it would take by hand, making it one of the most versatile tools in a modern kitchen. Whether you’re prepping soups, sauces, salsas, or doughs, a good one earns its counter space by turning tedious tasks into quick, consistent results. When shopping, focus on bowl capacity (most family-sized models hold 10 to 14 cups, but mini versions work well for singles or couples), motor power (higher wattage often means better performance on tough jobs like kneading bread dough), and ease of cleaning, dishwasher-safe parts are a huge timesaver. Also consider included blades and discs; a shredding disc and a dough blade add real value, while a storage case keeps everything organized.
